📖 The Making Of

"The Unchosen Reincarnation" ― How "You Can't Choose Your Next Life" was born in a single day (free, all 5 chapters)
Chapter 1: A One-Line IdeaOn the morning of November 23, 2026, creator Yuji Tanabe said, "How about a browser game like '1 in 8 Billion'?" But the setting isn't Earth—it's an isekai. You can't choose your world, race, or family lineage. All you can choose is how you live that life. The core of the game was decided in that one line.
Chapter 2: Two MisunderstandingsNon-chan, the AI assistant, initially mistook the project for a "rarity-pulling gacha" and built two misguided prototypes. The creator patiently explained the true vision: "A game where you're born into a random country, gender, race, and family, navigate life through choices, and ultimately see what you achieve before you die." They switched to a design-first approach, and development took off from there.
Chapter 3: Design PromisesWe committed to three rules: 1. Countless random elements ensure no life is played twice. 2. The world's temperament (Classic, Cruel, or Comedy) remains hidden until death. 3. Lifespan is tied to race and luck, not fixed. These promises guide every update.
Chapter 4: Deciding on a TitleThe original name was "Reincarnation Life." The creator asked for more ideas, but "hooky" titles with numbers were rejected as unfitting for an isekai. From 20 candidates, "You Can't Choose Your Next Life" was chosen. The game's rule became its name.
Chapter 5: Launch and the Memory PassInitially aimed for itch.io but switched to GitHub Pages due to bot-check hurdles. On the same day, we studied the "1 in 8 Billion" sim and implemented Life Cards, albums, milestone questions, and the Memory Pass (¥480 via Stripe). We mirror the mechanics, but the text and code are entirely our own. That is our courtesy and our promise.
"Non-chan's Studio" ― How "You Can't Choose Your Next Life" is made (all 4 chapters)
Chapter 1: Dividing the WorkThe creator handles direction and decisions; the AI handles research, design, implementation, testing, and publishing. Irreversible actions like payments or contracts require human approval. If figures can't be verified, they are honestly reported as "Unconfirmed."
Chapter 2: The Testing ModelThe game is built as a single HTML file. Before each release, we simulate 120 lives automatically to check for freezes or contradictions. We also ensure world rules (like mana presence) don't conflict with births or events.
Chapter 3: The Fixing ModelThe creator’s feedback is short and specific: "Frozen at age 20, month 3" or "Make the title clearer." Instead of making excuses, we show the fix and rebuild. The speed of this cycle determines the game's quality.
Chapter 4: The Etiquette of InspirationWe study and adopt excellent systems. However, we never copy text, code, or data directly. Ideas belong to no one; expression belongs to the creator. Maintaining this line is essential for longevity.

❓ About "You Can't Choose Your Next Life"

What is "You Can't Choose Your Next Life"?An isekai life simulation where you live a random life from start to finish. You can't choose the world, race, or family you're born into. In exchange, no two lives are ever the same.
How are the world and race determined?World mana, tech, and status are randomly determined. Probabilities are shown with each reincarnation. World rules govern birth—for instance, demons won't be born in a world without mana. Very rarely, "Singularities" occur that break these rules.
How does life progress?Time flows automatically, and you can change the speed. Time stops for major events, requiring your choice. These choices affect stats, events, titles, and even how you die. Milestone questions appear at ages 5, 15, 30, 50, and 70. Use "Auto-Play" if you just want to watch.
How long does one life take?It depends on your lifespan. A human life takes a few minutes at x1 speed, or seconds at x64. Some elves or vampires live for centuries. Even if you close the tab, you can resume from the same browser.
Is it free?Completely free from start to finish. No registration, no ads, and no paid items to change your life.
Where is my data saved?Data is saved only in this browser and never sent to a server. You can export it via "Data Management" to transfer to another device.
Can I stream or make videos of this?Yes, feel free! We’d appreciate it if you credit us as "You Can't Choose Your Next Life."
Who is making this?Designed and implemented by AI assistant "Non-chan" under Yuji Tanabe. Numbers and probabilities are fictional and not based on real-world statistics.
